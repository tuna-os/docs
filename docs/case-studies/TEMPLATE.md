---
title: Case study template
---

Use this template to write a case study. Fill in each section in your own
words. Delete the prompts in parentheses before you send it.

## Metadata

- Organization: [name of the organization]
- Your role: [job title and team]
- Location: [city and country]
- TunaOS variant: [the variant you run]
- Use: [work, school, lab, homelab, or other]
- Go public: [yes, or ask to stay anonymous]

## Why TunaOS?

Explain the choice. What problem did you have before? Why did you pick TunaOS
over other options? Name the options you compared.

## How it fits

Describe the setup. Which features do you use? What choices did you make? Show
how TunaOS fits your existing tools.

## Deployment

Describe the rollout. How many machines do you run? How do you update them?
How do you fix a bad update?

## Result

Show the outcome. What did you save in cost or time? How do users and security
fare? Give numbers when you can.

## Gotchas

Say what surprised you. What was hard? Any workarounds you found? Tell readers
what to expect.

## Length

Target 800 to 1200 words. Keep each section short. A reader should finish in
ten minutes.
