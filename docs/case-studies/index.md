Case studies show how real organizations use TunaOS. They give first-time
readers proof that others run TunaOS in production.

This program collects accounts from adopters in their own words.
An organization explains why it chose TunaOS, how it shipped the images, and what it learned.
TunaOS edits each account for length and clarity, then publishes it here.

## Who should submit

- Organizations that run TunaOS at work, in school, or in a lab.
- Teams that moved to TunaOS from another atomic desktop.
- Anyone who can describe a real deployment in plain words.

## How to submit

1. Read the [case study template](/docs/case-studies/TEMPLATE). It lists the sections every
   account covers.
2. Write your account in the template. Target 800 to 1200 words.
3. Send it one way:
   - Open a discussion in [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/tuna-os/tunaOS/discussions).
   - Email the team (address in the [README](https://github.com/tuna-os/docs)).
   - Open a PR to this repo if you are a community member.

We check claims about TunaOS before we publish. We edit for grammar and
length. We keep your words and remove nothing without your say-so. You may
ask to stay anonymous.

## What happens next

- We confirm you want to go public.
- We check every claim about a TunaOS feature or version.
- We edit for clarity and length.
- We publish the page and announce it.
